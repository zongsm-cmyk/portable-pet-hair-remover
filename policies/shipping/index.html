Shipping Policy

Our initial delivery markets are the United States and United Kingdom. Online ordering is currently paused while supplier availability, carrier pricing and delivery estimates are verified.

Before you pay

Each available product will display its delivery estimate, origin and total shipping charge. An estimate is not a guaranteed arrival date. We will not advertise a carrier, prepaid duties or express delivery without confirmation for that product and destination.

Processing and tracking

Once an order is accepted, we will provide its processing estimate and tracking information when available. Customs checks, remote destinations and carrier delays can affect delivery. Contact us if your shipment is late or missing.

Duties and taxes

Any known charges collected by the store will be disclosed before payment. Import charges collected by local authorities or carriers will be described where applicable. We do not currently promise duties-paid delivery.
