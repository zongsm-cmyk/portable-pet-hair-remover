VERVELIVING / CUSTOMER CARE

Returns & Refunds

For products bought through links on our guides, the retailer you purchased from handles returns and refunds. Contact that retailer using the order confirmation; do not send a return to VerveLiving.

Damaged or incorrect items

Contact the retailer promptly with your order confirmation and relevant photos, following its published support process.

Change-of-mind requests

Review the retailer's return window, eligibility and postage rules. Applicable mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Refund timing

The retailer and its payment provider determine the refund process and timing. For any historical order placed directly with VerveLiving, contact us with its order number for support.
