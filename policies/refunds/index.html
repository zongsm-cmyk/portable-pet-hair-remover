Online ordering is currently paused. For an existing order, contact support with the order number, delivery date and the reason for your request.

Requesting a return

Please contact us within 30 days after delivery to request a return for an unused item in its original condition. Obtain the correct return instructions and destination before sending anything. Do not send a return to a parcel's dispatch address without confirmation.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

Contact us promptly with photographs and your order details so we can assess a replacement or refund. This policy does not limit mandatory consumer rights that apply to your purchase.

Costs and refunds

For a change-of-mind return, the customer normally pays return shipping unless applicable law requires otherwise. For a confirmed faulty or incorrect item, we will provide an appropriate remedy and explain any return costs before you send it. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; processing time depends on the payment provider.
