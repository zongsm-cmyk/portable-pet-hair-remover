THE CARE NOTES / 001

How to remove pet hair from car seats.

A gentler routine for cleaning loose fur without damaging upholstery.

Three useful steps.

  1. Test first. Choose a hidden fabric area before working across a visible seat.
  2. Lift loose hair. Use gentle, short strokes on compatible materials. Avoid rough scraping.
  3. Finish the details. Collect the lifted hair and vacuum where appropriate. Avoid wetting seat electronics.

Follow your vehicle manufacturer’s upholstery care instructions.
